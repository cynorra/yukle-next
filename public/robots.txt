# A crawler that matches a named group here IGNORES the "User-agent: *" group
# entirely (robots.txt has no inheritance). Every named group below therefore
# repeats the full private-path list — a named group with only "Allow: /"
# would silently un-block /marketplace (scraped third-party contact data),
# /api and the account pages for that crawler.
