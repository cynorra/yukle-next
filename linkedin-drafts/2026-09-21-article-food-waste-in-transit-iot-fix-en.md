# Food Waste in Transit: Causes, Real Costs & The IoT Fix

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
Inconsistent trailer temperature and poor airflow cause hidden food spoilage even when reefer units run correctly.

Cold chain transport relies on maintaining proper conditions throughout transit, not just at the loading dock. Managing perishable cargo involves three operational factors:

- Monitoring multi-point trailer temperatures and humidity continuously
- Minimizing dwell times and reducing door openings during stops
- Ensuring correct pallet stacking to prevent blocked airflow

Real-time IoT monitoring provides continuous data visibility so teams can catch temperature deviations immediately rather than diagnosing issues after delivery.
```
