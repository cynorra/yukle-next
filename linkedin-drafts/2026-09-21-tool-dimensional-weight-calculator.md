# Dimensional Weight Calculator

```text
A 24 by 18 by 12 inch box produces 5,184 cubic inches and equals 37.29 pounds when divided by 139.

Shippers, e-commerce sellers, and carriers often need to determine whether a shipment is billed on actual weight or dimensional size. Chargeable weight is always the higher of the two.

- Dimensional weight equals length times width times height divided by the carrier divisor
- Common divisors include 139 or 166 for US parcel, 5000 or 6000 for air, and 1000 kilograms per cubic meter for sea
- Lower dimensional weight by using smaller boxes, less void fill, and comparing carrier divisors

This free browser calculator helps identify billing weight before handing freight to a carrier. Data entered remains private and is not stored.
```
