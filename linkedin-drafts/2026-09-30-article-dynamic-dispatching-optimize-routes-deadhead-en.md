# Dynamic Dispatching: Optimizing Routes and Reducing Deadhead

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
Dynamic dispatching uses real time data and predictive analytics to adapt routes as conditions change, reducing empty miles.

Static dispatching relies on rigid plans that fail during road disruptions, causing fuel waste, driver burnout, and higher operating costs.

- Integrate transport management systems, logging devices, and GPS trackers
- Define metrics like deadhead percentage and driver utilization before starting
- Pilot the strategy on a specific lane or subset of drivers first
```
