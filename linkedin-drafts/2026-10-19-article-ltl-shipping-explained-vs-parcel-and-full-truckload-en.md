# LTL Shipping Explained: How It Differs From Parcel and Full Truckload

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
Less-than-truckload shipping sits between parcel services and full trailers by sharing space across multiple stops.

Freight classification relies on specific factors before booking. Consider these operational checks when evaluating options:

- Confirm federal registration and check the minimum liability insurance level for non-hazardous freight.
- Request the liability limit and claims filing period in writing from the provider.
- Verify fees for accessorial services such as liftgates, inside delivery, and residential drops.
```
