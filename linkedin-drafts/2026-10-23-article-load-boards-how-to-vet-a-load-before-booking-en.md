# Load Boards: How to Vet a Load Before You Book It

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
A broker may not operate in a name other than the registered name or represent itself as a carrier on freight listings. 📦

Online load boards list freight posted by shippers, brokers, and carriers. Before booking, checking a few key details helps avoid legal and financial issues.

- Confirm the poster holds active federal registration.
- Verify the exact name on the rate confirmation matches official records.
- Calculate the rate per total mile by including empty miles driven to the pickup.

Each party to a brokered transaction holds the right to review the transaction records. Payment timing between brokers and carriers depends entirely on the written contract rather than standard leasing rules.
```
