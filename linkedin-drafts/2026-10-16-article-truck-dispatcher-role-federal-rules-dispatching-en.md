# Truck Dispatcher: What the Role Is and What the Federal Rules Say About Dispatching

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
Federal safety regulations do not contain a defined legal category for a truck dispatcher.

Instead, safety rules treat dispatchers within a motor carrier as agents or representatives whose decisions fall under the carrier's duties. When assigning freight, dispatchers must ensure loads comply with specific federal operating limits.

- Motor carriers must require observance of driver regulations and cannot treat limits as the driver's problem alone.
- Assigning loads that exceed allowable driving hours violates federal limits.
- Coercion rules prohibit forcing an impaired or fatigued driver to operate.
```
