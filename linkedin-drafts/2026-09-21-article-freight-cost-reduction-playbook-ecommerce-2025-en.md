# The Complete Freight Cost Reduction Playbook for E-commerce Businesses

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
Shipping costs take a significant share of revenue for online retailers, making freight management a major focus for margins.

E-commerce logistics operations often rely on specific strategies to control these expenses. Reviewing past invoices helps identify where funds are leaking before making structural adjustments.

- Audit shipping data across carriers, zones, and surcharges before changing operations.
- Use multiple parcel carriers and route shipments dynamically based on zone performance.
- Switch to less-than-truckload freight for heavier B2B shipments instead of parcel services.
- Optimize box sizes to reduce dimensional weight charges for large and lightweight items.
```
