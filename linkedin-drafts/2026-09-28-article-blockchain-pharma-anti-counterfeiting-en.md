# Blockchain for Pharma Anti-Counterfeiting: Verify, Secure, Comply

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
Blockchain technology creates an immutable record for pharmaceutical products, securing the supply chain from counterfeits.

Existing supply chain systems often lack end-to-end transparency, creating "black boxes" where product authenticity can be compromised. This makes tracing difficult and increases operational risks like costly recalls.

Blockchain provides verifiable trust through its core components:
- A distributed ledger replicates data across a network, making records tamper-proof.
- Cryptographic hashing secures each transaction, creating an unalterable history.
- Smart contracts automate compliance checks and can trigger actions like recalls.
- Serialization registers unique package numbers on the blockchain for integrity at each hand-off.

This foundational security reduces errors and mitigates losses from product diversion, directly impacting a logistics operation’s profitability.
```
