# The Store-to-Store Transfer Playbook: Cut Costs & Shrink

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
Store-to-store transfers often fail because outdated manual processes hide the true cost of moving inventory between locations.

Inefficient internal shipping wastes labor hours and creates hidden expenses beyond basic transportation fees. Retail supply chains benefit from specific operational improvements:

- Using regional high-capacity stores as temporary hubs to pool overstocked items
- Consolidating shipments instead of dispatching half-empty vehicles for single boxes
- Adopting digital tools to coordinate routing and inventory visibility
```
