Intermodal fits long hauls, but transloading prevents final mile delays. 🚚

Choosing between transload and intermodal requires evaluating specific cargo needs rather than relying on a single method.

- Intermodal works best for full container loads over long distances without strict delivery urgency.
- Transloading suits specialized cargo or freight needing final delivery via standard dry vans.
- Facilities can transfer goods between container types to match destination requirements and avoid equipment shortages.
