# Freight Factoring Explained: How Selling Invoices Works

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
Under uniform commercial code rules, selling an unpaid freight invoice means the seller retains no legal interest in the sold account. 🚛

Freight factoring lets motor carriers turn unpaid invoices into cash sooner by selling them to a third party. Understanding the mechanics helps dispatchers and shippers navigate agreements and notices.

- The commercial terms and fees come from the contract between the carrier and the factor.
- After receiving an authenticated notice of assignment, the broker or shipper must pay the factor to discharge the debt.
- Account debtors may request reasonable proof of the assignment before redirecting payments.
```
