# How to Check a Trucking Company Before You Hire It

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
An unrated carrier status does not mean unsafe, because the federal rule defines it only as a company that has not been assigned a safety rating.

Checking a motor carrier before tendering freight involves verifying several public items. Federal regulations outline specific standards for registration, insurance, and safety fitness.

- Confirm active registration and display of the USDOT number on self-propelled commercial vehicles.
- Check that the insurance endorsement and surety bond are issued in the exact name of the motor carrier.
- Verify safety ratings, which fall under satisfactory, conditional, unsatisfactory, or unrated categories.
```
