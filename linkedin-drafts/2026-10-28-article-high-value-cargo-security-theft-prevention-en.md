# High-Value Cargo Security: Preventing Theft and Mitigating Loss

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
Criminals use identity theft to pose as legitimate carriers and acquire loads through fraudulent means.

Protecting high-value freight requires a layered strategy that addresses vulnerabilities across the entire transit network. Freight operations can reduce exposure by focusing on several core areas:

- Conducting routine risk assessments to map entire transport routes and identify secure stopping points.
- Verifying carrier operating authority and checking insurance coverage before releasing any high-value cargo.
- Using tamper-resistant physical seals that require specialized tools to remove and provide clear evidence of unauthorized access.
```
