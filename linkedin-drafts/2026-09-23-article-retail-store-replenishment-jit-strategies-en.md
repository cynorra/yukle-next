```text
Just-In-Time delivery slashes retail inventory holding costs by minimizing safety stock and leveraging technology for predictive reordering.

Traditional logistics often relies on keeping excess inventory in warehouses, which creates financial drains and operational issues for carriers. Shifting to agile replenishment strategies improves overall efficiency.

- Integrate Point-of-Sale data directly with warehouse systems for immediate reorder triggers
- Leverage predictive analytics to account for local events and weather patterns
- Establish dynamic safety stock thresholds by item and location

Implementing these methods reduces empty return miles and fuel expenditures while ensuring product availability.
```
