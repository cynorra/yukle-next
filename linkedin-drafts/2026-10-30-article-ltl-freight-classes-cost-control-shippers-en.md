# LTL Freight Classes: Unlocking Cost Control for Shippers

## Post — copy everything in this block

```text
Accurately classifying freight prevents unexpected rebills and budget overruns in less-than-truckload shipping operations.

Unpredictable shipping expenses often stem from a mismatch between declared freight classes and the carrier assessment upon inspection. These carrier corrections frequently include administrative fees and retroactive cost differences.

The classification system evaluates four transportability factors:
- Density, measuring the ratio of shipment weight to volume
- Stowability, assessing how easily commodities load with other freight
- Handling, evaluating the equipment required to load and unload
- Liability, considering risks of theft, damage, or perishability
```
