## Full description
Loadly is a live load board connecting shippers and carriers worldwide. Browse active truck loads and backhauls by route, weight, and trailer type (dry van, reefer, flatbed), post your own freight, and message carriers directly - all in real time.

✓ Live load board - thousands of active loads updated daily
✓ Backhaul and deadhead-free loads for dry van, reefer, flatbed, and box truck
✓ Search by origin, destination, truck type, and weight
✓ Direct messaging between shippers and carriers
✓ Freight guides, rates, and route insights in 54 languages
✓ Built for owner-operators, carriers, and freight brokers

Whether you're a carrier looking for your next load or backhaul, or a shipper who needs a truck fast, Loadly's load board puts real freight in front of real drivers - no middleman required.

Download Loadly and start finding loads today.

## Keyword rationale
Primary term: **"load board"** — the exact phrase the US trucking industry searches (matches how DAT and Truckstop.com are found on Play Store), not a generic "freight marketplace." Secondary: "freight", "carrier", "shipper", "truck loads" — all repeated naturally, no stuffing.

Update 2026-09-15: en-US is the template every other locale was localized FROM, but it was missing two things every localized version later added on its own — named equipment types (the ES/PT/IT/PL/RU versions all list "dry van, reefer, flatbed"-equivalents; en-US just said generic "trailer type") and "backhaul"/"deadhead" — the single most commonly searched real term among US owner-operators for a return load / avoiding empty miles (equivalent to "viaje en vacío" in es-ES, "попутный груз" in ru-RU). Both added.
